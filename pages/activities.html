Activities

Selected milestones, conference contributions, special sessions and seminars from our research lab.

July 2026 - Emanuele Nicoletti presents at PETS 2026

Emanuele Nicoletti presented Training TFHE-Based Neural Networks with Approximated Floating-Point Arithmetic at the Privacy Enhancing Technologies Symposium in Calgary, Canada, held on July 20–25. The work was developed with Fabrizio Pittorino, Alessandro Falcetta, Luca Colombo and Manuel Roveri. Read the paper or visit the symposium website.

June 2026 - Two special sessions at IJCNN / WCCI 2026

The lab organised special sessions on Tiny Machine Learning and Privacy-Preserving Machine and Deep Learning at IJCNN, part of WCCI 2026, held in Maastricht, the Netherlands, on June 21–26. Massimo Pavan and Manuel Roveri organised the TinyML session; Manuel Roveri and Alessandro Falcetta organised the privacy-preserving learning session. The sessions covered efficient learning on constrained devices and methods for protecting data during training and inference. More information: TinyML session and privacy-preserving learning session.

May 8, 2026 - PhD defenses for Luca Colombo and Matteo Gambella

Luca Colombo and Matteo Gambella successfully defended their PhD theses at Politecnico di Milano. Luca's thesis, From a Real Present to a Binary Future: Algorithmic and System Co-Design for Privacy-Preserving Deep Learning Training, addressed efficient and privacy-preserving training. Matteo's thesis, Neural Architecture Search in Constrained and Dynamic Environments, explored automated architecture design for real-world deployment. The examination committee was chaired by Giacomo Boracchi, with Elisabetta Fersini and Roberto Vezzani as external examiners. Congratulations to both!

April 2026 - BEP presented at ICLR in Rio de Janeiro

Luca Colombo presented BEP: A Binary Error Propagation Algorithm for Binary Neural Networks Training at ICLR 2026, held in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, on April 23–27. Developed with Fabrizio Pittorino, Daniele Zambon, Carlo Baldassi, Manuel Roveri and Cesare Alippi, the work investigates training binary neural networks using binary error signals and bitwise operations. See the conference website.

January 2026 - Three lab papers at AAAI in Singapore

Three contributions from the lab were accepted at AAAI 2026, held in Singapore on January 20–27: ReBoot: Encrypted Training of Deep Neural Networks with CKKS Bootstrapping, DQT: Dynamic Quantization Training via Dequantization-Free Nested Integer Arithmetic and InfoQ: Mixed-Precision Quantization via Global Information Flow. Together they address encrypted training, integer arithmetic and efficient quantization. Luca Colombo presented ReBoot, developed with Alberto Pirillo. See the conference website.

January 2026 - Neural Architecture Search in the AI Excellence programme

Manuel Roveri and Matteo Gambella delivered sessions for EY professionals as part of the AI Excellence programme at POLIMI Graduate School of Management. The sessions explored Neural Architecture Search and hardware-aware model design, including the trade-offs between accuracy, memory and energy consumption on edge devices. Practical demonstrations introduced automated model development using Teachable Machine.

October 2025 - NITRO-D presented at ECAI in Bologna

Luca Colombo presented NITRO-D: Native Integer-Only Training of Deep Convolutional Neural Networks at ECAI 2025 in Bologna, Italy. Developed with Alberto Pirillo and Manuel Roveri, the work studies neural network training entirely in the integer domain, with applications to TinyML and homomorphic encryption. See the conference programme.

June–July 2025 - Conference presentations and session chairs at IJCNN

Alessandro Falcetta, Fabrizio Pittorino, Matteo Gambella, Massimo Pavan and Hazem Hesham Yousef Shalby participated in IJCNN 2025 in Rome, held from June 30 to July 5. The team's presentations included HEDEL, DYNAMAX and TActiLE. Massimo Pavan chaired the Tiny Machine Learning special session, while Hazem co-chaired the workshop Eyes of the Future: Integrating Artificial Intelligence in Smart Eyewear.

Feb. 2025 - PhD Defense for Alessandro Falcetta and Massimo Pavan

Big milestone for the AI-Tech Research Lab as Alessandro Falcetta and Massimo Pavan wrap up their PhDs. Their work in Privacy-Preserving Machine and Deep Learning and Tiny Machine Learning, respectively, has been an important part of what we do, and we're glad to have been part of their journey. The discussion, which took place on February 18, was chaired by Professor Giacomo Boracchi, with Prof. Eiman Kanjo and Dr. Manish Gupta serving as committee members. A lot of hard work, a lot of late nights, and now—onto whatever's next. Well done!

Jan. 2025 - Seminar: Alternative Algorithms for Deep Learning: From Neuroscience to Technology

On January 27, 2025, Prof. Fabrizio Pittorino held a seminar titled Alternative Algorithms for Deep Learning: From Neuroscience to Technology in the Emilio Gatti Conference Room at Politecnico di Milano, as part of the FAIR - Future Artificial Intelligence Research series. The seminar explored alternatives to backpropagation at the intersection of neuroscience and artificial intelligence. More information on the event page.

Dec. 2024 - Special Sessions at IJCNN 2025

The lab announced two special sessions for IJCNN 2025: Privacy-Preserving Machine and Deep Learning and Tiny Machine Learning. These initiatives brought together research on data privacy and machine learning for resource-constrained devices.
